## Project Overview

Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and needs to understand how her business is performing using records from the last 14 days. The analysis examines daily and weekly sales performance, identifies sales trends, compares weekday and weekend performance, classifies daily sales, and evaluates stock levels and product gross margins. The objective is to provide practical, data-driven recommendations to help Wanjiku make better sales, inventory and restocking decisions using core Python.

## How to Run

Open the analysis notebook in Google Colab: [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/19RmzZTntUcpn_DQO4MVv4iGzYWxcjKnc?usp=sharing)

## Top Three Findings

1. **Weekend sales performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900 compared to weekday sales of KES 2,685, representing an 82.50% increase.

2. **Positive weekly sales growth:** Sales increased from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2, representing 10.66% growth.

3. **Stock replenishment needs:** Tomatoes had 18 kg against a reorder level of 25 kg, while Sukuma had 9 kg against a reorder level of 15 kg. Both products require restocking.
